🐝 Save the Bees

Simple actions, lasting impact

Why Bees Matter

Bees and other pollinators are the foundation of healthy ecosystems and our food systems. Without them, our world would be vastly different.

🌍

Ecosystem Health

Bees pollinate 75% of the world's flowering plants. They maintain the balance of nature that all life depends on.

🌈

Biodiversity

Pollinators support diverse plant species, which in turn support countless animals, insects, and microorganisms.

🍎

Food Production

One in three bites of food you eat depends on pollinators. Fruits, vegetables, and nuts all rely on bees.

How You Can Help

Everyone can make a difference. Here are practical, easy steps you can take right now.

Let Your Garden Grow Wild

The most impactful action: reduce mowing and let natural vegetation flourish.

  • Stop mowing regularly—let grass grow tall and wild flowers emerge
  • Avoid pesticides and herbicides that kill the plants and insects pollinators need
  • Allow "weeds" to bloom—dandelions, clover, and other native plants are food sources
  • Create unmowed patches where insects can shelter and breed

A wild garden isn't messy—it's alive. Even a small patch of natural growth helps pollinators.

🌿
🌻

Plant Native, Nectar-Rich Flowers

Give pollinators food throughout the growing season.

  • Choose native flowers that bloom at different times of year
  • Spring bloomers: crocuses, bluebells, pulmonaria
  • Summer bloomers: lavender, coneflowers, zinnias
  • Fall bloomers: asters, sedum, goldenrod
  • Avoid hybrid flowers with no pollen or nectar

Native plants are adapted to your climate and need less water and care.

Build an Insect Hotel

Provide safe nesting and shelter for solitary bees and beneficial insects.

  • Fill a wooden structure with hollow stems, straw, and twigs
  • Solitary bees nest in hollow tubes—they don't sting and are excellent pollinators
  • Creates shelter for ladybugs, lacewings, and other beneficial insects
  • Perfect for small gardens and balconies
  • DIY kits available, or make one from recycled materials

Building an insect hotel is a fun, rewarding project that makes a real impact.

Small Actions, Big Impact

Other simple ways to support pollinators:

  • Provide water: A shallow dish with stones gives insects a safe drink
  • Leave dead wood: Hollow stems and dead trees shelter many insects
  • Skip the chemicals: Stop using pesticides, fungicides, and herbicides
  • Plant densely: Clusters of flowers are easier for bees to find
  • Share your knowledge: Talk to neighbors about protecting pollinators

Start Today

You don't need a perfect garden—you need a wild one. Every unmowed patch, every native flower, every insect hotel makes a real difference.
